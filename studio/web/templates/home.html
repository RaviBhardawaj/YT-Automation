How it works

  1. Create your free account — the team approves new accounts quickly.
  2. Set up a channel — niche, language, style, and (optionally) your YouTube channel's @handle.
  3. Connect YouTube once — official Google sign-in; you choose the channel. Skip it to just download videos.
  4. Make videos — type what you want, paste a link, or upload footage. Preview, download or publish.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes during beta, with monthly limits per account. Paid plans with higher limits are planned — see Pricing.

Do you get my Google password?

No. YouTube is connected through Google's official sign-in; you can remove access any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Make sure what you upload is yours or licensed, and follow YouTube's rules for AI-generated and kids content.

Can I just download the videos?

Yes — every finished video, Short, subtitle file and thumbnail can be downloaded.
