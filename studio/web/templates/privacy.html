Privacy policy

Last updated {{ updated }}

This policy explains what {{ site_name }} (“we”) collects when you use the service, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email, your name if you give it, and your password stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Your content: files you upload, your requests, the scripts, voices, videos, thumbnails and subtitles made for you, your channel settings and your ideas.
  • YouTube connection: when you connect a channel through Google's sign-in, we keep a token that lets the service upload videos and read that channel's details and statistics. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Usage and technical data: what was produced and how much (for plan limits), error logs, and your IP address for security (such as limiting sign-in attempts).

How we use it

  • To make, store and deliver your videos, and to upload them to the YouTube channel you chose, at the time and visibility you chose.
  • To show your channel's growth and monetization progress to you.
  • To keep the service secure, enforce plan limits and fix problems.

We don't sell your data and don't use your content to advertise to you.

Google and YouTube data

{{ site_name }} uses YouTube API Services. Information received from Google APIs is used only to provide the features you use here, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. By connecting YouTube you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove our access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions or with “Disconnect” on your channel page.

Services that process your content

To produce videos, parts of your requests and content are sent to the AI and infrastructure providers the service uses: script and planning models (Ollama), voice generation (Sarvam AI), optional image and video generation (Google Gemini), YouTube (for uploads you approve) and Cloudflare (to deliver the website). Each processes the data only to provide its part of the service.

Who can see your content

Your channels, videos, uploads and ideas are visible only to your account. The service operator can access stored data when needed to run, secure or support the service.

Keeping and deleting data

You can delete videos, uploads and channels at any time; deleted files are removed from our storage. To delete your whole account{% if contact %}, email {{ contact }}{% else %}, contact the operator of this site{% endif %}. Deleting here doesn't remove videos already published on YouTube; manage those in YouTube Studio.

Children

Accounts are for adults (18+) who run YouTube channels. Channels marked “made for kids” are uploaded with YouTube's made-for-kids setting; we don't knowingly collect personal data from children.
