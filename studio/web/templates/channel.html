Setup, step by step

  1. Facebook Page. You need a Facebook Page for this channel (facebook.com/pages/create) and to be its admin.
  2. Instagram professional account linked to the Page. In Instagram: Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account (Creator or Business). Then link it: Facebook Page → Settings → Linked accounts → Instagram → Connect.
  3. Connect here. Click Connect Facebook & Instagram, sign in with Facebook, allow every permission asked (Pages and Instagram publishing), then pick the Page in the list that appears.
  4. Turn on automatic publishing (left) — or publish videos one by one from each video's page.
Limits from Meta: Instagram up to 100 posts a day, Facebook Reels 30 a day; Facebook Reels must be vertical 3–90 seconds (longer videos go up as Page videos). While the platform's Meta app is in development mode, only people added to the app (Meta app → App roles) can connect.
